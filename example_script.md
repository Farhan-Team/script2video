# The Ocean

The ocean covers more than seventy percent of our planet. Its waves have shaped coastlines for millions of years, carving cliffs and building beaches grain by grain. Scientists believe most of the ocean is still unexplored.

[search: city skyline at night] Meanwhile, cities never sleep. Lights flicker across skyscrapers as millions of people go about their evening, connected by a web of roads, rails and signals.

High in the mountains, the sunrise paints the peaks in gold. "It's breathtaking," one climber said. The air is thin, cold and silent.

Forests breathe for the whole world. Trees store carbon, shelter wildlife and cool the land beneath them.
